Audience

This article is written for those, who struggle to understand the rationale behind certain Inverter Registers.

The Meaning of « Yesterday »

Most Inverters have a Register called Energy Today and a Register called Energy Yesterday.

If you wonder why this article is for you.

« Today »

« Today » has one interesing quality. It is not complete until it is over and when it is over it is not anymore « Today », but becomes « Yesterday ».

« Yesterday »

« Yesterday » has the interesting quality. It holds the value of « Today » the moment « Today » becomes complete.

Conclusion

Therefore « Yesterday » is not a random quirk by the people who wrote the software of the Inverter.

But the holder of « Today »

How is Energy Yesterday Useful?

Energy Yesterday is useful, because it allows the reading of the Energy Produced by the Inverter without a dedicated Power Meter.

Power Meters are not inexpensive and the ability to read the Energy Produced by the Inverter from the Inverter reduces the cost of the Installation.
